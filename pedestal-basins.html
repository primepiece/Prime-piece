The Details

Made to install
properly.

A pedestal basin is a serious piece of stone. Here's what to know before your plumber and cabinet maker start planning.

Standard size
Approx. Ø430 × 850mm round, or 400 × 400 × 850mm square. Custom heights and diameters available, e.g. Ø400mm.
Finish
Honed (matte) or polished. Honed is our recommendation for marble basins, as it hides everyday wear best.
Drain
Matching stone drain cover included.
Plumbing
Hollow core so the waste can run down through the pedestal. Designed for wall-mounted tapware, with no tap hole in the basin.
Weight
Natural stone is heavy. We confirm the weight with your quote so the floor and fixing can be planned.
Lead time
Typically 6–8 weeks from deposit. Plan around Chinese New Year if your build is due in February or March.
Delivery
Crated and insured, delivered NZ-wide. Auckland delivery or collection from Wairau Valley.
Care
pH-neutral cleaner only. Seal every 12 months with a penetrating stone sealer.

Common Questions

Pedestal basins,
answered.

How long does a pedestal basin take?+

Typically 6–8 weeks from deposit. Factories close for several weeks around Chinese New Year, so if your build is due in February or March, it pays to order early.

Can I choose my own size?+

Yes. Our standard is approximately Ø430 × 850mm (or 400 × 400 × 850mm square), but heights and diameters can be adjusted to suit your vanity, tapware or ergonomics. Include your preferred size in your brief.

Is a drain included?+

Yes. Pedestal basins come with a matching stone drain cover. Your plumber will connect the waste through the hollow core of the pedestal.

What tapware do I need?+

Pedestal basins are designed for wall-mounted tapware, as there is no tap hole in the basin. Check spout reach and height with your plumber before ordering.

Do you have non-marble options?+

Yes. Travertine and limestone give a warm white or cream look, and quartzite is harder and more resistant to etching. Ask for these in your quote.

Do you offer trade pricing?+

Yes. Designers, builders and developers can apply for trade pricing, including repeat designs across multiple builds. See our Trade Program or select "Builder / developer" in the quote form.
