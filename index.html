01 — Process

From quarry
to lounge.

Every Prime Piece moves through three hands: the quarry in Spain or Italy, the stonemason's bench in Auckland, and your front door.

one

Source

Stone is hand-selected at quarries in Spain and Italy. Every slab is chosen for movement, colour and character — not for uniformity, but for presence.

two

Carve

Shaped over days, sometimes weeks, by hand at our Auckland workshop. Seven years as a stonemason informs every cut. No production line. No duplicates.

three

Deliver

Wrapped, crated, and delivered white-glove across New Zealand. Each piece arrives with its own certificate of origin and care instructions.

02 — Story

Weight.
Texture.
Presence.

"I wanted to make furniture that felt ancient and modern at the same time — something you could inherit."

Prime Piece began with a simple idea. Transform natural stone into sculptural tables that celebrate the material's raw beauty.

Each piece starts with stone carefully selected from quarries in Spain and Italy, chosen for its movement, colour and character. Years working as a stonemason shaped the vision: pieces that bring permanence and weight into a modern space.

Every Prime Piece is one of one. Carved from natural stone. Ensuring no two pieces are ever the same.
