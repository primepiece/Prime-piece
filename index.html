The Craft

Every piece begins with natural stone — selected, cut, shaped and finished by hand in our Auckland workshop. Nothing is mass-produced, and no two pieces of stone are ever identical. Natural variation ensures no two pieces are ever exactly alike.

The Maker
James
Hopwood
Founder / Prime Piece

James Hopwood is an Auckland-based stonemason and founder of Prime Piece, a sculptural stone furniture brand creating one-of-one plinths and tables handcrafted in New Zealand.

Working with natural stone sourced from leading suppliers around the world, each piece is cut and finished by hand — shaped to celebrate the stone's own movement and texture, and never repeated.
