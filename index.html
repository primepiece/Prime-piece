The Maker
James
Hopwood
Founder / Prime Piece

James Hopwood is an Auckland-based stonemason and founder of Prime Piece, a sculptural stone furniture brand creating one-of-one plinths and tables handcrafted in New Zealand.

Working with carefully selected natural stones sourced from Italy, Spain and beyond, each piece is designed to celebrate the movement, texture and permanence of the material.

Drawing from years of hands-on experience in stone fabrication, his work explores the intersection of sculpture, furniture and interior presence.
