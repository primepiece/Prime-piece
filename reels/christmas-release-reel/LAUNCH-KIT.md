## How the release opens (no action needed after the merge)

The site opens the release on a timer:
- **Before Sat 14 Nov, 7pm:** the Christmas page shows the teaser and the first-look sign-up form.
- **Sat 7pm to Sun 7pm:** only people who came through the first-look email link can add boards to the cart and check out. Everyone else still sees the teaser.
- **From Sun 15 Nov, 7pm:** open to everyone. The boards also join the Google Shopping / Pinterest product feed.

Each board can only sell once. When one sells it shows Sold straight away, and so does the set.

**To pause or open it early:** in `lib/release.js`, set `RELEASE_OVERRIDE` to `'off'` (paused) or `'on'` (open to everyone now). This needs a deploy, so tell Claude and it can be done in a couple of minutes.
