Prime Piece creates handcrafted marble coffee tables and natural stone furniture in Auckland, New Zealand. Each piece is cut from a single slab of natural stone — sourced from Italy, Spain and beyond — and finished by hand in our Wairau Valley workshop. Our collection includes marble coffee tables, stone side tables, large-format lounge tables and custom dining tables. Stones include Rosso Africano, Carrara, Mystique Green, Verde Apli, Travertine, Kangaroo Quartzite, Sahara Noir and more. Every piece is one of one — once sold, it is gone forever. Custom stone furniture is available to commission in any stone, any size, with 4–8 week lead time. We deliver across Auckland and New Zealand.
