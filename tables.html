Common Questions

Everything you need
to know.

Are your marble tables one of one? +

Yes — every table at Prime Piece is cut from a unique slab of natural stone. No two pieces of marble, quartzite or granite are the same. The pattern you see in the photos is the exact piece available. Once it sells, it's gone.

Do you deliver across New Zealand? +

Yes, we ship nationwide. Auckland orders can also be collected from our Wairau Valley showroom. Delivery is quoted based on your location and the weight of the piece — just enquire and we'll sort it.

What is the difference between marble, quartzite and granite? +

Marble is a classic — elegant veining, slightly softer, best for decorative use. Quartzite is incredibly durable and heat resistant, ideal for high-traffic areas. Granite is the hardest of the three — dense, heavy and practically indestructible. All three are natural stone sourced from quarries around the world.

Can I get a custom size? +

Yes. We can commission custom pieces in different sizes, stone types and frame finishes. Lead time is typically 3–6 weeks depending on the stone. Enquire via the button below or send us a WhatsApp and we'll work through the details with you.

How do I care for a marble coffee table? +

Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid acidic cleaners (vinegar, lemon) as these can etch the surface over time. We recommend sealing marble annually with a stone sealer to protect against stains. Quartzite and granite require much less maintenance.
