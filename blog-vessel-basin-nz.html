How to choose a vessel basin for your bathroom renovation in NZ

By Prime Piece August 2026 8 min read

A vessel basin is one of the highest-impact upgrades you can make in a bathroom renovation — and in natural stone, it becomes something truly extraordinary. This guide covers everything New Zealand homeowners need to know before choosing one.

What is a vessel basin?

A vessel basin (also called an above-counter basin or countertop basin) sits on top of the vanity rather than being recessed into it. Unlike undermount or inset basins, the vessel sits fully visible — making it a design feature in its own right, not just a functional fixture.

In natural stone, this format is particularly powerful. The full form of the stone is on display: the curves, the veining, the depth of colour. It reads as sculpture as much as bathroom hardware.

The key difference from a standard basin: A vessel basin is a statement. It changes the visual hierarchy of the entire bathroom — drawing the eye upward and making the vanity feel intentional rather than incidental. Choose it when you want the bathroom to have a focal point.

Sizing: what you need to know

All Prime Piece vessel basins are made to a standard Ø 430mm diameter × 135mm height. This is the industry standard for above-counter basins and has been chosen to work with most standard vanity depths (typically 450–600mm).

Vanity height considerations

This is the most important practical question when choosing a vessel basin. Because the basin sits on top of the vanity rather than inside it, your effective counter height increases significantly.

  • Standard comfortable height: The top rim of the basin should sit between 850–900mm from the floor for most adults
  • With a 135mm basin: Your vanity surface should be approximately 720–770mm from the floor
  • Check your existing vanity: Standard NZ bathroom vanities run 820–850mm tall — this is often too high for a vessel basin and may require a custom or lower vanity

Pro tip for renovations: If you're doing a full renovation, plan the vanity height around the basin, not the other way around. Commission your vanity at 720mm and the basin top rim will sit at a perfect 855mm — ergonomically ideal for most adults.

Installation: what's involved?

Natural stone vessel basins are heavier than ceramic alternatives — each Prime Piece basin weighs approximately 12–18kg depending on the stone. Installation is straightforward but requires some preparation.

What your plumber needs to do

  • Install a standard 40mm centre drain hole in the vanity surface (drain fitting purchased separately)
  • Position the waste pipe and water supply at the appropriate height for an above-counter installation
  • Ensure the vanity surface can bear the weight of the stone basin — most solid timber and stone vanities are fine; avoid hollow particleboard

Does it need to be sealed?

Yes — all natural stone basins should be sealed before use and resealed annually. We recommend a quality stone sealer (available at Bunnings or most tile suppliers). Marble is more porous than granite and benefits particularly from regular sealing. This is a 10-minute job once a year and significantly extends the life and stain resistance of the stone.

What does a natural stone vessel basin cost in NZ?

Stone vessel basins in New Zealand range from approximately $800 to $2,500+ depending on the stone type, origin, and manufacturer. Prime Piece basins start from NZD $800 for White Carrara and Nero Marquina, with premium and rare stones like Statuario and Pink Purple Onyx reaching NZD $1,050–$1,499.

For context, a quality ceramic vessel basin from a NZ bathroom supplier typically runs NZD $400–$800. The premium for natural stone is real — but so is the difference in visual impact and longevity. Natural stone, properly cared for, will outlast the rest of the bathroom.

Marble vs onyx vs travertine: which is right for your bathroom?

Marble

The most versatile choice. Works in contemporary, Scandi, traditional, and coastal bathrooms alike. Carrara and Nero Marquina are the most timeless options — they've been used in architecture and interiors for centuries and never date. Marble does require sealing and is susceptible to etching from acidic products (think citrus, vinegar, certain cleaning agents) — choose pH-neutral cleaners.

Onyx

Onyx is a more dramatic, more luxurious choice than marble. It's semi-translucent, which means backlit onyx has a quality that no other stone can match. It's also slightly softer than marble and requires more careful maintenance. The visual payoff is exceptional for a statement bathroom.

Travertine

Warm Beige Travertine has an earthy, organic quality that suits Mediterranean and resort-inspired bathrooms particularly well. It has a natural texture and occasional surface variation that gives it character marble doesn't have. Slightly more porous than marble — sealing is particularly important.

Frequently asked questions

Can a vessel basin be used in any bathroom?

Yes, with the right vanity setup. The main consideration is vanity height — you'll want your vanity surface at around 720mm to achieve a comfortable rim height of 855mm with our basins. If your existing vanity is 820mm+, a vessel basin will sit uncomfortably high.

How long does a natural stone basin last?

Indefinitely, with proper care. Natural stone has been used in bathrooms for thousands of years. The key is regular sealing (once per year) and avoiding acidic cleaning products. A well-maintained stone basin will look better at 20 years than it did at installation as the patina develops.

Is the drain fitting included?

No — the drain fitting is not included with Prime Piece basins and should be purchased separately from your bathroom supplier or plumber. A standard 40mm pop-up waste fitting is what you need.

Can I see the exact basin before ordering?

Because each basin is made from a unique piece of natural stone, the exact veining and colouration will vary from the photos shown. If you have a specific requirement, contact James directly — we can often provide photos of the specific slab before cutting begins.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes — all basins ship nationwide within New Zealand. Enquire for delivery timeframes to your region.
