Policy

Returns & Exchanges

Our Commitment

Every Prime Piece is handcrafted from natural stone in our Auckland workshop. Because each piece is one of one, we take great care to ensure it reaches you in perfect condition. If something isn't right, we want to hear from you.

Returns

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery or pickup, provided the item is in its original condition and has not been damaged after receipt.

  • Contact us within 14 days at james@primepiece.co.nz or 021 146 6990
  • Items must be returned in original packaging or equivalent protective wrapping
  • Return freight costs are the responsibility of the buyer unless the item is faulty or damaged in transit
  • Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the item being received back

Defective or Damaged Items

If your piece arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, we will arrange collection and offer a full refund or replacement at no cost to you. Please photograph the damage on arrival and contact us immediately.

Exchanges

We are happy to discuss exchanges where possible. Because our pieces are one of one, a direct swap may not always be available — but we will always try to find a solution. Get in touch and we'll work something out.

Made to Order & Custom Pieces

Custom-commissioned pieces are non-refundable once fabrication has begun, as they are made to your specific requirements. We will always confirm all details with you before starting work.
