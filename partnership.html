Stone furniture made

Prime Piece creates one-of-one handcrafted furniture and objects in marble, quartzite, porcelain and natural stone. Each piece is singular — no two are the same. We are looking for a small number of select Auckland spaces to showcase work, collaborate, or commission custom pieces for premium interiors.

What we work with
Marble
Calacatta, Nero Marquina, Statuario and more. Timeless, high-contrast, singular.
Quartzite
Harder and more durable than marble. Dramatic veining, made for daily use.
Porcelain
Large-format slabs with consistent patterning. Refined surface for contemporary spaces.
Natural Stone
Basalt, travertine and raw stone forms. Honest materiality, built to last generations.
How we work together
Consignment
Furniture & Homeware Stores
We place pieces in your showroom. You take a percentage on sale only — no upfront cost, no risk. We retain ownership until purchased.
Trade Partnership
Interior Designers & Architects
Specify our pieces for client projects and receive trade pricing. We handle fabrication, delivery and installation. Custom briefs welcome.
Placement
Hotels, Restaurants & Hospitality
A piece lives in your space for an agreed period. We handle insurance and logistics. You have the option to purchase at any time.
Commission
Developers, Galleries & All
We create a bespoke piece to a brief. Material, scale and form are developed together. Suitable for lobbies, show homes, exhibitions and permanent collections.
