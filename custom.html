The Process

From conversation
to delivery.

A personal process, start to finish. You'll deal directly with James from first conversation through to delivery.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Tell us about your space, the piece you have in mind and any stone you love. A photo of the room helps. We'll be in touch to discuss your piece.

  2. 02

    Design & refine

    Together we refine the stone, dimensions and details until the piece is right for your space.

  3. 03

    Confirm

    You'll receive a clear quote and timeframe for your piece. A 50% deposit commences production.

  4. 04

    Handcrafted in Auckland

    Your piece is individually fabricated and finished by experienced Auckland stone craftsmen. Most custom pieces are handcrafted within 2–3 weeks from confirmed order.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    The remaining balance is due before delivery. Auckland delivery is arranged personally, with freight available throughout New Zealand.
