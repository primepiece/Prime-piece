The Collection

Plinths.

Six pieces — each one of one

Sourced from the quarries of Italy and Spain, then shaped entirely by hand at our Auckland workshop. No moulds. No repetition. Every piece carries the natural movement, tone, and character of the stone it was born from.

Stone is not manufactured — it is found. We select each block for its individual character, then let the material lead the form.
