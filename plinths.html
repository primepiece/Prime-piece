Prime Piece crafts handcrafted marble plinths and natural stone pedestals in Auckland, New Zealand. Each plinth is carved from a single block of natural stone — Rosso Africano from Africa, Rosso Levanto from Spain, Natural Travertine, Navarro Marble, Green Calacatta, Dekton Sirius and more. Our marble plinths are used as sculptural display pedestals, plant stands, side tables and art platforms. Every piece is one of one — no two are ever the same. Custom stone plinths are available to commission in any stone, any height and any finish. Made by hand in our Wairau Valley workshop, Auckland. We ship across New Zealand.
