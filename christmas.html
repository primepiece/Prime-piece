Gift questions

Good to know.

Will it arrive before Christmas?

Order by the dates above and it will. We confirm your delivery timing personally after you order — if you're cutting it close, text James on 021 146 6990 before you buy.

Can you send it directly to the person I'm buying for?

Yes. At checkout choose NZ-wide delivery, enter their address, and tick “This is a gift”. We wrap it, add your handwritten card, and leave the prices out.

How much is delivery?

$49 flat rate anywhere in New Zealand, free on orders of $500 or more. Collection from our Wairau Valley showroom is always free.

Is every board different?

Yes — each one is cut from natural stone, so the colour and veining you see in the photo is the piece you get. Nothing is repeated.

How do they look after a stone board?

Wipe clean with warm water and mild soap, dry it, and avoid leaving acidic things like lemon or wine on the surface for long. More in our serving board guide.

Can I see them in person?

Come by our showroom at 209B Archers Road, Wairau Valley, Mon–Fri 8am–5pm. Free on-site parking.
