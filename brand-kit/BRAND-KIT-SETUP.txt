PRIME PIECE BRAND KIT
Taken from primepiece.co.nz (site CSS) and the official logo files.

01-Logos   Official logo (unchanged artwork) in teal, cream and charcoal, transparent PNG,
           plus 1080x1080 profile images on ivory and charcoal.
02-Fonts   Cormorant Garamond, Inter Tight, Dancing Script. All SIL Open Font License,
           free for commercial use and embedding (licences included).
03-Colours Palette card, hex list, type specimen.

SET IT UP IN CANVA (about 5 minutes; needs a team owner, admin or brand-designer role)
1. Canva home > Brand Hub > Brand Kit > create a new kit called "Prime Piece".
2. Logos: upload the three prime-piece-logo-*.png files.
3. Colours: add a palette called "Prime Piece" with the hex codes in 03-Colours/palette.txt.
4. Fonts: Brand fonts > Upload a font > select all 11 .ttf files in 02-Fonts.
5. Set the text defaults:
     Heading     Cormorant Garamond Light
     Subheading  Inter Tight Medium   (use UPPERCASE with wide letter spacing for eyebrows)
     Body        Inter Tight Regular
6. Restyle an existing design: open it > click the background > Styles > pick the
   Prime Piece text-style tile. Every font in the design switches at once.

HOW THE SITE USES THEM
  Headlines     Cormorant Garamond Light, slightly tight letter spacing
  Accent        Dancing Script Medium in deep sage, for "one of one" moments only
  Body          Inter Tight Light/Regular
  Buttons       Inter Tight Medium/SemiBold, UPPERCASE, wide letter spacing
  Logo teal #54ABBB is for the logo; use deep sage #5D8588 for text accents and buttons.
