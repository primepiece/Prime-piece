# Gift voucher marketing designs: rebuild spec

These 10 designs were first built in a Canva account that isn't Prime Piece's. This file holds everything
needed to rebuild them in Prime Piece's own Canva with the brand kit in this folder.

**Rules for every design**
- Fonts: headlines in Cormorant Garamond Light (slightly tight letter spacing). Eyebrows in Inter Tight
  Medium, UPPERCASE, wide letter spacing. Body text in Inter Tight Regular. Dancing Script Medium in deep
  sage only for "one of one".
- Colours: ivory `#F5F1EA`, charcoal `#2C2A26`, deep sage `#5D8588`, soft sage `#A8C3C5` (accent on
  charcoal), stone `#8A8275`.
- Vouchers: use the exact artwork (`/voucher-card-100.jpg`, `-200`, `-500` on primepiece.co.nz, 5:3).
  Never crop, recolour or redraw them. Light cards get a 1–2 px `#CFC6B6` border and a soft offset shadow
  (charcoal at 13% opacity, offset 8–14 px). The dark $500 card gets a stone border on dark backgrounds.
- Overlapping vouchers: the left card sits on top, and each overlap covers at most ~36% of the next card
  (its marble strip), so every value stays readable. Stacked vertically, each overlap stays under ~33%.
- No clip-art, no gift boxes or ribbons, no physical cards: these are digital vouchers, emailed instantly.
- URL: `primepiece.co.nz/gift-vouchers` (corporate: `primepiece.co.nz/gifts.html#corporate`).
