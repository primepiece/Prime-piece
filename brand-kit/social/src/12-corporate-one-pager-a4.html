Corporate gifting
Thank your clients with something one of one.
Prime Piece gift vouchers let your clients and staff choose their own handcrafted stone piece, from a $99 marble serving board to a one-of-one marble table. Every voucher shows a different stone from our Auckland workshop.

How it works

  1. Tell us how many vouchers and which values. Mix $100, $200 and $500 as you like.
  2. We send you a quote and an invoice.
  3. Vouchers are emailed to you or straight to each recipient, ready to forward. Printed stone cards are available too.
  4. They spend it on any piece, online or in our showroom.

The details

  • Values: $100, $200 or $500 NZD
  • Valid for 3 years from purchase
  • Spend on anything, online or at the Wairau Valley showroom
  • Change comes back as a new voucher, so nothing is lost
  • Printed cards: the voucher's stone on heavy card, your message written by hand
Good for: client thank-yous · staff Christmas gifts · project handovers for builders, designers and architects · referral thank-yous
