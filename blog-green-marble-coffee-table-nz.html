Green marble coffee tables in NZ: choosing your green

By Prime Piece November 2026 5 min read

Green stone does something white marble can’t: it brings colour into a room without paint, cushions or a rug having to do the work. It’s also the stone our customers ask about most. Here are the greens we work with, how to tell them apart, and the pieces available now.

The greens we work with

Verde Imperiale
Deep forest green with explosive white veining. The boldest green we use, and the most dramatic on a dark frame.
Feels: bold · moody · high contrast
Green Calacatta
A white Calacatta base streaked with deep green and black. The lightest way into green: mostly white, with colour in the veins.
Feels: light · fresh · easy to style
Green onyx
Translucent jade green with crystal formations that glow when light hits them. The rarest stone in our collection, and softer than marble, so it’s best for a living room rather than a high-traffic spot.
Feels: luminous · rare · jewel-like
Mystique Green
Cool jade, sage and teal tones crossed with golden and copper fault lines that shift through the day. The stone behind our larger lounge tables.
Feels: calm · warm metallics · organic
Verde Alpi
Deep, dark green with natural white movement. Reads almost black from across the room and green up close.
Feels: grounded · classic · rich
India Green
Deep forest green with dark veining and an earthy texture.
Feels: earthy · textured · dark

Styling a green table

  • Warm timber floors and linen: green stone and oak or rimu are a natural match. Cream, oatmeal and olive linen let the stone lead.
  • Brass and black steel: brass picks up the gold veining in Mystique Green; black steel sharpens Verde Imperiale.
  • Plants, but not too many: one large plant nearby echoes the stone. A whole jungle competes with it.
  • Keep the top simple: a single bowl or a stack of books. The stone is the decoration.

Frequently asked questions

Is green marble durable enough for a coffee table?

Yes, with the same care as any marble: use coasters, wipe spills promptly and avoid acidic cleaners. Every Prime Piece top is sealed with two coats of nano-top sealer before it leaves the workshop, and we recommend resealing once a year. Green onyx is softer than marble, so it suits a living room better than a high-traffic area.

What is the difference between green marble and green onyx?

Green marble, like Verde Imperiale or Green Calacatta, is opaque with strong veining. Green onyx is translucent, with crystal formations that glow when light passes through them. Onyx is rarer and softer, so it needs a little more care.
