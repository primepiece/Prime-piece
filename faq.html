Marble is prized for its veining and warmth — it can etch with acidic liquids over time but remains the most visually distinctive choice. Granite is harder and more resistant to staining. Quartzite looks similar to marble but is significantly harder and more durable in daily use. For lounge and coffee tables with regular use, quartzite and granite are more forgiving; for sheer visual impact, marble is hard to beat.
Use a soft cloth and pH-neutral cleaner — avoid acidic products such as vinegar or citrus-based sprays. Seal marble annually with a quality stone sealer and wipe spills promptly, especially red wine, coffee and citrus juice. Granite and quartzite are more forgiving but the same principles apply. We include care instructions with every piece.
