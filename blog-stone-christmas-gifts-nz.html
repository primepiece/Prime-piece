Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who has everything in NZ?

Something handmade and one of a kind. A natural stone serving board ($99) or entertaining platter ($189) is made from a single piece of marble or onyx, so nobody else will have the same one. If you are unsure of their taste, a gift voucher lets them choose, and it is emailed instantly.

When do I need to order for delivery before Christmas?

Order by 15 December for delivery anywhere in New Zealand before Christmas. After that you can collect from the Wairau Valley showroom on weekdays, or buy a gift voucher, which is emailed instantly with no cut-off.

Are marble serving boards food safe?

Yes. Natural stone is non-porous once sealed and completely food safe. Wipe it with warm soapy water after use and avoid acidic cleaners like vinegar or citrus, which can dull marble over time.

Can I use a gift voucher on the Christmas Release?

Yes, gift vouchers work on the Christmas Release boards. Discount codes do not apply to those three boards, and Afterpay is available at checkout.
