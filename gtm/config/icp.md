# Prime Piece GTM: Ideal Customer Profile, Offer & Voice

Every GTM agent reads this file first. Edit it when the business changes. The agents only know what's written here.

## What we sell

Prime Piece is an Auckland studio (showroom: Wairau Valley) making one-of-one handcrafted furniture and objects in natural stone (marble, quartzite, onyx, travertine). Furniture stone comes from Italy and Spain.

| Line | Typical retail (NZD) | Lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee & side tables | $200 – $2,800 | 2–3 weeks (custom) |
| Plinths / pedestals | $700 – $7,750 | 2–3 weeks |
| Serving boards & platters | gift price points | stock |
| Stone vessel & pedestal basins | ~$1,450 – $1,500+ | 6–8 weeks |
| Custom commissions | quoted | 2–3 weeks furniture / 6–8 weeks basins |
| Gift vouchers | $100 / $200 / $500 | instant |

## Trade program (the B2B offer, see `/trade.html`)

- Preferential trade pricing on all collections, stock and custom
- Priority lead times, scheduled around the project
- **Complimentary photorealistic renders** of a piece in the client's space
- Physical stone samples couriered to the studio
- Direct access to James, with no account manager
- First right of refusal on new / limited stone
- Spec documentation: datasheets, care guides, dimensions, install guidance
- No minimum order, NZ-wide delivery, white-glove in Auckland

Other offers already in use:
- **Placement / consignment**: a piece lives in a hotel, restaurant, gallery or showroom at no cost, with an option to buy; showrooms take a % on sale.
- **Corporate gifting**: boards, platters and vouchers for client/staff gifts (strongest Oct–Dec).

### Hard fit criteria
- Based in New Zealand (Auckland first, then Wellington, Queenstown, Christchurch, Tauranga).
- Works at the premium end (luxury residential, boutique hospitality, $1.5M+ property).
- A real decision-maker is reachable (owner, director, principal, FF&E lead, GM).

### Disqualifiers
Volume/budget fit-out, mass-market retailers, businesses outside NZ (for now), anyone who has opted out. Also any firm that is a direct competitor in stone furniture.

## Personas

- **Principal / Director**: cares about client outcome, reputation and margin. Short emails, a visual hook, social proof.
- **Interior designer / FF&E lead**: cares about lead time, samples, renders, spec docs and reliability.
- **Hotel GM / ops**: cares about guest experience, zero hassle and budget. Placement works well here.
- **Showroom buyer**: cares about sell-through and margin. Consignment works well here.

## Voice (all copy)

- Written as **James**, founder, first person. Warm, plain, confident. No hype or exclamation marks.
- Short: first-touch emails under 120 words; LinkedIn notes under 300 characters.
- Lead with *their* project or space, not with Prime Piece.
- One clear, low-friction ask: a quick visit, a render of their space, samples, or a lookbook.
- Use NZ English spelling (colour, specialise, centre).
- Never invent facts, clients, awards or prices. Only use what's in this file or verified research.

## Compliance guardrails (non-negotiable)
1. **NZ Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007**: commercial email needs consent. Inferred consent is OK only when the address is *conspicuously published* (website/LinkedIn) **and** the message relates to the recipient's business role. Every email must identify James/Prime Piece and include an opt-out line ("Not relevant? Just reply 'no thanks' and I won't follow up."). Honour opt-outs immediately → stage `do_not_contact`.
2. Never email a guessed/pattern address unless it has been verified, or James approves it.
3. **LinkedIn**: agents *draft* connection notes and messages only. James sends them by hand, because automation breaks LinkedIn's terms.
4. **Nothing is sent automatically.** Emails are saved as Gmail drafts for James to review and send.
5. Store only business contact info. No personal phone numbers or home addresses.
