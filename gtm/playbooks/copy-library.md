# Copy library: structures & offer angles

The Copywriting Agent's reference. Examples show **structure and tone**; never copy them word for word. Every real email is built from that account's research hooks.

## First-touch structure (≤120 words)
1. **Hook** (1 sentence): their project, opening, award or post. Specific and verifiable.
2. **Bridge** (1–2 sentences): why a one-of-one stone piece fits *that*.
3. **Proof** (1 sentence): a concrete detail (Italian/Spanish stone, made in Auckland, 2–3 week custom lead time, renders).
4. **Ask** (1 sentence): a single low-friction step.
5. Signature + opt-out line.

## Offer angles (match to `research.recommended_offer`)
| Offer | Angle | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| `render` | "See it in the space before anyone commits" | "Send me a photo of the space and I'll render a piece in it, no charge" |
| `samples` | Stone has to be seen in person, since veining varies slab to slab | "Can I drop a few samples to the studio this week?" |
| `trade-account` | Trade pricing, priority lead times, direct line to the maker | "Worth setting up a trade account for upcoming projects?" |
| `basin-spec` | Natural stone vessel/pedestal basins, 6–8 weeks, spec docs ready | "Happy to send the basin trade PDF and spec sheets" |
| `placement` | A statement piece in their space at no cost, with the option to buy | "Could I bring a piece in to show you?" |
| `consignment` | One-of-one range extension, no upfront cost, % on sale | "Could I bring a piece in so you can see it properly?" |
| `corporate-gifting` | Client gifts that get kept: stone boards/platters, vouchers | "Want me to put together a few options for your client gifts?" |

## Example: interior designer, render offer (illustrative)
> Subject: stone for the herne bay project
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> Saw the Herne Bay living room you shared last week. The travertine you paired with the oak joinery is lovely.
>
> I make one-of-one coffee tables and plinths in Auckland from Italian and Spanish stone, and I think a piece could sit really well in a space like that. For trade clients I'll do a photorealistic render of a piece in the actual room, so your client can see it before anyone commits.
>
> If you've got a current project where that'd help, send me a photo and I'll mock something up.
>
> Kind regards,
> James
> Prime Piece | primepiece.co.nz
>
> Not relevant? Just reply 'no thanks' and I won't follow up.

## Follow-up (≤80 words): add something new
A new angle, not "just bumping this". Options: a specific stone that suits their palette, a recent piece photo, a lead-time window ("I've got capacity for two custom pieces before Christmas"), or a relevant project type.

## Close the loop (step 4)
> Hi Sarah, I don't want to clutter your inbox, so this is my last note. If a render or samples would ever help on a project, I'm a reply away. — James

## LinkedIn connection note (≤300 chars)
> Hi Sarah, I really enjoyed the Herne Bay project. I make natural stone furniture in Auckland and love following NZ interiors work. Would be great to connect.

## Never
"I hope this email finds you well" · "quick question" bait · fake "Re:" subjects · invented mutual connections · urgency tricks · more than one link · attachments in first touch
