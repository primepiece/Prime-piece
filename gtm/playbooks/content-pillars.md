# Content pillars (LinkedIn & X)

Audience: NZ interior designers, architects, developers, stylists, hospitality owners. Goal: by the time an email lands, they've seen James and the work.

| Pillar | What | Example hooks |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Stone stories** | Origin, geology and character of specific stones | "Every slab of Calacatta is different. Here's why we never photograph a 'sample'." |
| **2. Maker's process** | Cutting, finishing, weight and handling, behind the scenes | "This plinth weighs 140kg. Getting it into a Ponsonby apartment took…" |
| **3. In the space** | Finished pieces in real rooms, before/after, renders vs reality | "Render on the left, install on the right." (`before.jpg` / `after.jpg`) |
| **4. Designer utility** | Practical spec advice: care, sealing, lead times, basin install tips | "5 things to check before specifying a stone vessel basin" |
| **5. Founder POV** | Building a stone studio in Auckland, lessons, opinions on NZ design | "Why I stopped doing 'collections' and only make one-of-one" |
| **6. Trade proof** | Trade program benefits shown through outcomes, not adverts | "A designer sent me a phone photo on Monday. Render back Wednesday, client signed Friday." |

Rules: one detail per post that only a maker would know. No invented numbers. Ask permission before naming a client or project.
