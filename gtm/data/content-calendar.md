### Thu 26 Nov · LinkedIn · Pillar 2 Maker's process
Image: `release-set.jpg` (all three boards stacked) · Post: 7:45am
Before posting: check which Christmas Release boards have sold. If all three are gone, swap the last line for "All three have found homes. Thank you. If you missed out, the first-look list hears about the next one first."

> The slowest part of making this year's Christmas boards wasn't the cut.
>
> Three grazing boards, three stones: burgundy red, deep green, and white with grey veining. Each is 600 x 250mm, big enough for a long Christmas lunch.
>
> Cutting them to size is the quick part. The time goes into polishing: rounding the corners and bringing the edges up until they feel right in your hand. Then each board is signed and numbered on the back, "Christmas Release 2026 · 1 of 3".
>
> There won't be a fourth, and there's no restock. When a piece of stone is used, that's it. It's the same way I approach furniture commissions: one slab, one piece, never repeated.
>
> Which would you put on your table: the red, the green or the white?
>
> #NaturalStone #MadeInNZ #OneOfOne
