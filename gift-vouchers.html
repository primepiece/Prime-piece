Voucher questions

Can it be used on anything?

Yes, on any ready-made piece online or in the showroom. It can't be used to buy another voucher or exchanged for cash.

What if they spend less than the voucher?

The change is emailed to them straight away as a new voucher code, so nothing is lost.

Is there a Christmas cut-off?

No. Vouchers are emailed, so you can buy one right up to Christmas Eve and it arrives in a minute.

How long is it valid?

3 years from the day you buy it. The expiry date is printed on the voucher.

Can I buy one in the showroom?

Yes. Pop in Monday to Friday, 8am–5pm, at 209B Archers Road, Wairau Valley, or call James on 021 146 6990.
