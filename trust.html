Caring for Stone

Natural stone is built to last generations. A little care goes a long way to keeping your piece looking exactly as it did the day it arrived.

Clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners.
Seal marble annually — we recommend a quality stone sealer to protect against staining.
Wipe spills immediately — especially acidic liquids like wine, coffee or citrus on marble.
Use coasters and mats under items on polished surfaces to avoid scratching.
Basalt and granite are more forgiving — highly durable and resistant to everyday wear.
