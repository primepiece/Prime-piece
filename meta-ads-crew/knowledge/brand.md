# Prime Piece — brand facts for ads

Source of truth for every desk. If a claim isn't here or on the live product page, it doesn't go in an ad.

## Who we are
- Sculptural natural-stone furniture and objects, cut and finished by hand in Auckland (Wairau Valley workshop + showroom, 209B Archers Road, Mon–Fri 8am–5pm).
- Founder: James Hopwood, Auckland stonemason. Customers deal with James directly, not an account manager.
- Stone from Italy and Spain: marble, onyx, travertine, granite, quartzite, basalt.
- Every piece is one of one. The photo shows the stone variety; the customer's piece has its own veining (say this, don't hide it).

## Don't say
- "Signed" / "numbered" / "limited edition of X". Removed from the site on purpose.
- Any % off or discount code unless James confirms an offer is live this week. (Discount codes don't apply to the Christmas Release.)
- "Cheapest", "best in NZ", invented review quotes, invented customer names, fake scarcity. Real scarcity is fine: each piece is literally one of one.
- Delivery promises beyond the page (Christmas cut-off: order by 15 December for NZ-wide delivery).
- Health/safety claims about stone (food-safe etc.) beyond "sealed, use for serving not chopping".

## Voice
Calm, confident, plain. Short sentences. Talk about the stone and the room it goes in. No hype words ("insane", "game-changer"), no emoji walls, at most one emoji. NZ spelling (colour, centre). Prices in NZD.

## Who buys
1. **The considered home-owner**: 30–60, Auckland/Wellington/Christchurch, renovating or furnishing a new build, buys one good thing instead of three average ones.
2. **The gift buyer**: boards and vouchers, Nov–Dec and Mother's/Father's Day spikes.
3. **The interior designer / architect / builder**: trade pricing, wants a reliable maker and stone before it's public.
4. **The bathroom renovator**: vessel basins, comparing against generic imported basins.
