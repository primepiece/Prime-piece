## Formats that work for high-AOV craft (starting points, unproven for us)
- **Specific object + specific room**: "One slab of Rosso Levanto. One side table. Nothing else in the room needs to try as hard."
- **The question they're already asking**: "Is marble too high-maintenance for a coffee table?"
- **Counter-intuitive**: "The cheapest part of this table is the labour."
- **Maker POV**: "I cut this from a single slab. There won't be another."
- **Proof first**: "Zero returns. Every review five stars. Here's why."
- **Price anchor** (only with a real, checkable comparison): "Italian marble coffee tables, cut in Auckland, from $490."
- **Gift reframe**: "Every candle gets regifted. Nobody regifts a slab of onyx."
- **Room diagnosis**: "If your lounge feels unfinished, it's probably missing one heavy thing."
