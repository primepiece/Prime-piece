# Angle library: Prime Piece

Sam (strategy) scores these against each brief. An **angle** is *why someone buys*, and a **hook** is *how the first line stops the scroll*. One angle can carry many hooks.

**Facts the crew may use** (from the site; don't invent others):
- Handcrafted one-of-one natural stone pieces, made in Auckland, NZ
- Showroom at 209B Archers Road, Wairau Valley, Auckland, where buyers can see slabs in person
- Made-to-order pieces are handcrafted within 2–3 weeks of a confirmed order (lead times vary with complexity and stone availability)
- Custom sizing and commissions available
- Gift vouchers in $100, $200 and $500, emailed instantly and valid for 3 years
- Ranges: coffee tables, side tables, plinths, vessel and pedestal basins, serving boards and platters

Anything else (delivery terms, warranties, discounts, review counts, "bestseller") must be confirmed by James before it goes in an ad. Mark it `[VERIFY]`.

Score each angle 1–5 per brief on: **Fit** (true for this product?), **Pain/Desire strength**, **Proof available** (do we have the image or fact?), **Freshness** (not already fatigued in `../03-creative-desk/hooks.md` results). Sam ships the top 3–5.

## A. Uniqueness / one-of-one
1. **No one else will ever own this exact stone.** One slab, one piece.
2. **Pick your actual piece**, not a SKU: the photo is the table you get.
3. **Anti-mass-produced:** "Your friends have the same coffee table from the same big-box store."
4. **Nature designed the pattern.** Veining formed over millions of years.
5. **Collector framing:** stone pieces as heirlooms, not furniture.

## B. Local / NZ-made
6. **Handcrafted in Auckland**, not shipped flat-packed from overseas.
7. **Meet the maker:** James and the workshop (founder-led trust).
8. **See it before you buy:** visit the Wairau Valley showroom and touch the slab.
9. **Support local craft** vs offshore marketplace knock-offs.
10. **Kiwi homes:** pieces styled in real NZ living rooms and bathrooms.

## C. Transformation / the room
11. **One piece changes the whole room** (before/after: `before.jpg` → `after.jpg`).
12. **The statement piece your living room is missing.**
13. **Bathroom glow-up:** a stone vessel basin is the cheapest way to make a bathroom feel designed.
14. **Renovation finisher:** the last 10% that makes a reno look architect-led.
15. **Small space, big impact:** side table or plinth for apartments.

## D. Quality / material
16. **Real stone vs "marble look"** laminate and sintered imitation.
17. **Weight = quality:** feel the heft.
18. **Lasts a lifetime.** Buy once and it outlives three sofas.
19. **Care is easy** (from the marble-care blog): bust the "marble is high-maintenance" myth.
20. **Material education:** onyx vs marble vs travertine, and which suits you.

## E. Custom / control
21. **Your size, your stone:** custom dimensions for awkward spaces.
22. **Commission story:** from slab to finished piece (process photos).
23. **Choose the slab in person,** then we make it.
24. **Designers and trade:** spec a one-off for a client project (`trade.html`).
25. **Made-to-order basins** that match your vanity exactly.

## F. Price / value framing
26. **Designer look without designer markup:** compare with European stone furniture prices `[VERIFY comparisons]`.
27. **Cost per year:** a $X table over 20 years.
28. **Entry piece:** start with a $189 serving board and own real stone today.
29. **Price-anchored range:** boards → side tables → coffee tables.
30. **Clearance / last pieces** (only when there really is one, e.g. lounge clearance).

## G. Gifting / occasions
31. **The gift they'd never buy themselves** (boards, platters).
32. **Let them choose:** gift vouchers, emailed instantly, valid 3 years.
33. **Last-minute gifting:** a voucher lands in their inbox seconds after you pay.
34. **Housewarming / wedding:** a serving board with a story.
35. **Seasonal entertaining:** summer grazing boards (Christmas release).

## H. Social proof / identity
36. **Customer homes:** real installs (`commissions.html`, `review-simone.jpg`).
37. **"Do you know where that's from?"** The piece guests ask about.
38. **Taste signal:** for people who notice materials.
39. **Interior-designer approved** `[VERIFY: only with a named designer's permission]`.
40. **Behind the scenes:** cutting, polishing, the workshop sound and process reels.

## I. Urgency (honest only)
41. **This slab is the only one.** When it sells, it's gone.
42. **Drop / new arrivals:** new basin colours just landed.
43. **Production window:** order by date X for delivery by Y `[VERIFY dates each time]`.

## J. Objection killers
44. **"Will it fit?"** Custom sizing plus a showroom visit.
45. **"Is it fragile?"** Natural stone durability and sealing.
46. **"Can I see it first?"** Yes, at the Wairau Valley showroom.
47. **"How long does it take?"** About 2–3 weeks handcrafted (made-to-order).
