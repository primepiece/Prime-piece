## Rules
- First line ≤ 8 words and readable without sound.
- One idea per ad. Don't stack three angles.
- Write in Prime Piece voice (see `voice.md`): calm, confident, material-first. No ALL CAPS and no "🔥🔥🔥".
- Never claim a discount, delivery promise, review count or award that isn't in the facts list in `../02-strategy-desk/angles.md`.
