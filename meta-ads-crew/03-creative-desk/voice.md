# Prime Piece voice (for ad copy)

Source of truth: `brand-kit/copy/COPY-PACK.md`. Read it before writing.

- **Calm, confident, material-first.** The stone does the talking.
- Short sentences. Plain words. NZ spelling (colour, favourite, jewellery).
- Light emoji at most (🤍 is on-brand). Never use hype stacks.
- Talk about *the piece* and *the home*, not "our products".
- Specifics beat adjectives: "Teal Tide onyx, 60 cm" beats "stunning luxury table".
- Close with one clear action: shop the piece, visit the showroom, or choose a voucher.

**Primary text shape** (≤ 600 chars, ideally ~125 before "See more"):
```
[Hook line]

[1–2 lines: the angle, with one concrete fact]

[Proof / detail line]

[CTA line]
```
**Headline:** ≤ 40 chars, the piece name or the promise. **Description:** optional, ≤ 30 chars.

**Images:** use the repo's real photos (`.jpg`/`.png` only, since the Meta image upload only reliably accepts those). Lifestyle shots (`lounge-*.jpg`, `lifestyle-*.jpg`) usually beat cut-outs for cold traffic; cut-outs suit retargeting and catalogue. Charlie picks; the creative *call* is yours.
