Gift questions

How does the two-board price work?

Add any two serving boards to your cart and they come to $179 instead of $198. It's applied at checkout automatically, and works for every pair, so four boards are $358.

Will it arrive before Christmas?

Boards ship anywhere in New Zealand for $49, or free on orders of $500 or more. If it's for Christmas, order early in December, or collect from the showroom on a weekday. Not sure about timing? Call James on 021 146 6990.

How do gift vouchers work?

Pay online and the voucher code is emailed to you straight away, so there's nothing to ship and no Christmas cut-off. Forward the email or print it for a card. The code works at checkout or in the showroom for 3 years, and if they spend less than the voucher, the change comes back as a new voucher.

Can I pay with Afterpay?

Yes, Afterpay is available at checkout.

Will it look exactly like the photo?

The board you receive is the one in the photo. Natural stone can look slightly different in person depending on the light, which is part of its character.
